Introduction

I Know Why You're Here

You're here because you've fallen into the trap that a lot of men fall into. You met a woman who was going through it, and somewhere along the way you decided you were the one who could save her.

I'm not here to clown you for that. I'm here for you, because I've done the same thing. More than once.

The urge to rescue is part of who we are as men. We see something broken and we want to fix it. We see someone struggling and we want to carry the weight. That instinct isn't the problem. The problem is pointing it at a project that can only be finished from the inside.

This book explains why it almost never works, what actually happens when you try, and what to do instead the next time a broken woman walks into your life asking for your attention.

You think you can change her. You think she's different. I've tried it at least three times.— King's Wisdom

Chapter One

You're Not the Exception

Men have been warned about this for centuries, in many different languages. The old saying is blunt: you can't turn a hoe into a housewife.

Here's what's wild. A lot of the men who know that saying, who'd repeat it to their boys without blinking, turn around and try to do exactly that.

Why? Because every one of them believes he's the exception. Sure, it doesn't work for most guys, but my situation is different. She's different with me.

In fact, those men aren't the exception. They are the rule. The man who thinks he's the exception is the most common man in this story.

Why the exception feels so real

It feels real because, at the start, you are getting a different version of her. She's showing you her best side because she wants something from you. More on what that something is in Chapter Four. For now, understand that being treated differently in the beginning isn't proof that the ending will be different.

The bottom line

If your plan depends on you being the exception, you don't have a plan. You have a hope.

Chapter Two

King's Wisdom's First Law

If you remember any physics, there's a law called Newton's First Law: an object at rest tends to stay at rest. It keeps doing what it's doing until something acts on it.

I'm going to give you a law of my own.

King's Wisdom's First Law

A broken woman usually stays broken.

How a woman becomes broken

It usually starts with an initial phase of trauma. Something in her environment hit her hard and impacted her negatively. That part isn't her fault.

What determines where she ends up is the second part: how she handles it. And in a lot of cases, she handles it poorly.

That's the important distinction. Trauma happens to everybody in some form. Brokenness is what happens when the response to that trauma makes things worse instead of better, and nobody ever goes back to fix it.

Like Newton's object at rest, a broken woman stays broken until something acts on her. As you'll see in Chapter Five, the only force that can actually do that job is her.

The bottom line

Trauma is what happened to her. Brokenness is how it was handled. You can't go back and handle it for her.

Chapter Three

The Wrong Fix for Every Problem

As long as I can remember, when it comes to internal problems, a lot of women are terrible problem solvers. They reach for the same fix every time: they go through a hoe phase.

Don't believe me? Let's break it down.

  • Drug problem → hoe phase.
  • Drinking problem → hoe phase.
  • Daddy issues → hoe phase.
  • Overweight → hoe phase.
  • Depression → hoe phase.
  • Broke → hoe phase.

Different problem, same answer, every single time.

Why it makes everything worse

Here's the issue: the hoe phase doesn't solve the problem. It compounds it.

When the phase is over, she still has the same drug problem, the same drinking, the same daddy issues, the same weight, the same depression, the same empty bank account. Now she also has a higher body count, which, the way I see it, lowers her value. She comes out the other side worse off than when she went in.

It doesn't solve the problem. In fact, it compounds the problem.— King's Wisdom

The bottom line

A fix that leaves the original problem in place and adds a new one isn't a fix. It's a detour that costs her more each time she takes it.

Chapter Four

What She's Really Looking For

So why do women choose a hoe phase to deal with what's going on inside? Contrary to popular belief, it's not about the sex.

At the core, what she's actually trying to do is find a man to solve her problems.

She leads with the wrong thing

The issue is that she goes about it the wrong way. She leads with her sexuality, and what does that get her? Used and left. Then she does it again, and again, phase after phase, until she's tired of it.

If she led with “I like you. I want you in my life,” a man would be far more likely to invest in her and actually help her solve her problems. But a lot of women don't know how to approach a man like that, so they fall back on the one tool they know gets attention.

Even when she finds the man, it doesn't hold

Here's the funny part. Even when she does find a man who steps up and tries to solve her problems, it still doesn't work.

As you and I both know, when you try to help a woman change her ways, it isn't a permanent solution. It's a band-aid. It covers the wound while you're there holding it in place.

The moment that relationship is over, notice what happens: she goes right back to the bad habits she already had. Nothing was fixed. It was only managed, by you, for as long as you were around.

The bottom line

If the change only lasts as long as you're there enforcing it, you didn't change her. You just became her band-aid.

Chapter Five

Reality, Accountability, Work

So what's the real solution? Here's the truth of the matter: the only person who can fix a broken woman is the woman herself.

Why can't broken women just fix themselves, then? Because it requires three things that, in my experience, women hate:

1. Reality

She has to accept that she is broken. She has to look at it and understand it first.

2. Accountability

She has to own the fact that the only one who can fix her being broken is her. Not you, not the next guy.

3. Work

Then she has to actually do the work of fixing it, day after day.

That may not sound like a lot to you. To her, it's a mountain.

The easier road

So what's much easier for her to do? Find a man to fix it.

That's the reason behind King's Wisdom's First Law. A broken woman usually stays broken because she never looks inside. She's looking everywhere else. The internal problems never get fixed. They just get smothered under promiscuity.

The First Law, explained

She stays broken because she never looks inside.

Ask yourself
  • Has she ever named her problem out loud without blaming someone else for it?
  • Has she ever taken a real step to fix something when nobody was making her?
  • Or is the plan, spoken or not, that you are the fix?

Chapter Six

When You Win and Still Lose

Let's say you are the exception. A woman comes to you wanting to change, wanting to be better. You take her under your wing and turn her into the best version of herself she can be.

You know what happens? Nine times out of ten, it's going to end badly.

Her value goes up. So do her options.

When a woman believes her value has increased, she believes she has access to better options. You were the one who invested in her when she was at her lowest, and that's exactly the problem. Now she thinks: I can do better than you.

She won't just leave. She'll make you leave.

She can't simply dump you, because that would expose that she's not actually a better person, and her new picture of herself would come crashing down into reality. As we covered, she hates reality.

So what does she do instead? She makes you want to go. She gets annoying. She gets cold and nasty. She starts resenting you. She becomes insufferable, so that you leave and she's free to chase the better options she now believes she deserves.

And you don't leave

But you've poured so much time, energy and resources into her that you aren't going anywhere. You invested too much. You're planted.

So now she's being insufferable, you're refusing to leave, and you're both miserable. All because you tried to turn a hoe into a housewife.

The weight-loss example

If you still don't believe me, picture this. You date a heavy woman, somewhere in the 250–260 pound range. You motivate her, you support her, and she drops a serious amount of weight.

Do you think you get to enjoy the benefits of her new body? No. She leaves you for somebody new, because she believes her value is higher now and she deserves better than the man who was with her when she was at her worst.

Only men value investment

Here's the hard part to swallow. If a woman built you up, if she was there when you had nothing and put in the time to help you become who you are, you would never leave her. You'd appreciate that investment because you know how hard it was.

It doesn't work the other way around. She'll leave if she believes her value has risen. That's hypergamy: the drive to trade up.

Only men value investment. Women do not.— King's Wisdom

The bottom line

Success at saving her raises her value in her own eyes. Your investment becomes the reason she leaves, not the reason she stays.

Chapter Seven

Your Three Options

So, King's Wisdom, what do I do when one of these broken women comes my way? You've got three options.

This applies when a broken woman comes looking for your attention and looking for something serious and long-lasting.

Option 1: Ignore her

You're not interested. Simple. No explanation owed.

Option 2: Keep it physical and keep moving

Keep it casual and walk away before you start investing. No saving, no project.

Option 3: Send her to fix it first

The one I like. It's only for when she has some real redeeming qualities.

How Option 3 works

If there's nothing redeeming there, it's not worth it. Go back to Option 1 or 2. But if she has some real redeeming qualities, tell her something like this:

I appreciate what you're doing. Go fix your issues, then come back. And if you do, we can see where this goes.

About 98% of the women you say that to will never come back, because, once again, a broken woman usually stays broken.

But the other 2%, the minority of the minority, give you a much better foundation to build on. Now you know she can fix her own internal problems. She proved it without you holding the band-aid. That is a very valuable woman to have, provided the internal problems weren't too severe to begin with.

The bottom line

Don't be the repair shop. Be the reward at the end of the repair.

Chapter Eight

In Closing

I know, I know. You think you can change her. You think she's different.

Good luck to you, my guy. But I've tried it at least three times, and every single time it failed, even when I thought I understood women.

I didn't get burned. I didn't get ruined. I didn't get destroyed. But I still failed. And anything that is not a success is a failure.

So please, don't waste your time. When a broken woman shows up looking for a savior, you have three moves:

  1. Ignore her.
  2. Keep it physical and keep moving.
  3. Tell her to come back once she's fixed her issues.

Simple as that.
