Introduction

This One's for You

I usually give men advice. Today I'm talking to you, the woman, and only you.

You've told me I'm too hard on women. Fair. So this time I'm on your side of the table. I'm going to tell you what men actually think, the stuff we say to each other and never say to you, so you can stop guessing and start choosing.

I'm going to be straight with you, because nobody else will be. Your girls will hype you up. Social media will tell you you're perfect as you are. The guys in your DMs will tell you whatever gets them what they want. I'm going to tell you the truth, because you deserve to know how the game really works.

First I'll break the illusion. Then I'll give you the blueprint.

The options you think you have? They're not real. I'm going to explain why, and then show you how to get real ones.— King's Wisdom

Chapter One

The Illusion of Abundance

Be honest with yourself for a second. Do you feel like you have options? Like there will always be men who want you, no matter how old you get?

Most women believe that. And I get why. Look around:

Guys are all over me. My phone, my DMs, my guy friends waiting in line, men approaching me everywhere I go. Of course I've got options.

Sis, I'm going to say this with love: those options are not real.

It's not your fault you think they are. Women have been making this mistake for thousands of years, because from where you're standing, attention looks exactly like opportunity. It isn't. Let me show you the difference.

The bottom line

Attention is not the same as options. You can be surrounded by men and still have very few who would actually choose you.

Chapter Two

Wanting You Isn't Choosing You

Here's the core mistake: you're confusing sexual attraction with relationship attraction.

A man being interested in you doesn't make him a viable relationship candidate. Not at all. For men, it has never meant that.

How men really think

Men compartmentalize. When a man wants to sleep with you, that's what he wants. When a man wants a relationship with you, that's a completely different thing. Two separate boxes. A man wanting you in bed does not mean he wants to make you his wife.

So when you think, “He thinks I'm cute, so if things don't work out with my man I can always go to him,” stop. You don't even know if he likes you. He may just want to hook up. That's not a backup plan. That's nothing.

You already know this feeling

Think about the guy who's sweet to you, wants a relationship with you, would commit tomorrow, but you're just not attracted to him. Does he count as an option to you? Be honest. No. You'd never actually choose him. He's not a real option.

That's exactly how men see it from the other side. The man who wants to sleep with you but won't commit is your version of that guy. His wanting you is like saying water is wet. Of course he does. It doesn't hold any real value for you.

Remember this

A man offering sex and a man offering commitment are two different things.

Chapter Three

Do the Math

Let's put a little logic on it. How many options do you really have?

Picture a thousand men who find you attractive. Now run them through the filters that matter to you, if what you want is a real, long-term relationship.

1,000 find you attractive
~900 would sleep with you
~500 would actually date you
10 want to marry you
You only find 3 of them attractive

Some men will find you attractive and still pass. Of the ones who wouldn't, only some would take you on a real date. Of those, only about ten want to marry you. And of those ten, you only find three of them attractive.

Yes, you can find guys to hook up with. Yes, you can find guys to date you. But a man who wants you for the long term, wants to marry you, and is someone you like back? All those filters shrink the pool very, very fast.

The bottom line

Out of the thousand options you think you have, you only have about three real, long-term options that actually mean something. So treat them that way.

Chapter Four

It's Not a Replenishing Resource

This part is hard to hear, so I'm going to say it gently and honestly. If you're in your mid-thirties to early forties, there's a good chance you've already met the men who could have been great partners for you.

If you're still single, it may be because you passed on them. Not because you're a bad person, but because you believed you had more options than you did. Maybe you turned them down, kept it casual, or let it fall apart. Those chances may be gone.

The mistake is thinking there are always more coming, like it's a replenishing resource. Unfortunately, for you it doesn't work like that.

Why it's different for you

For men, there's a woman turning twenty-one every single day. From his side, the supply never runs out.

For you, the older you get and the more money you make, the fewer men there are you'll consider. Your standards go up while the pool gets smaller. It gets harder and harder.

I'm not telling you this to scare you. I'm telling you so you can act on it. All is not lost. Here are four ways to increase your real, quality, long-term options, starting today.

Way One

Get in Shape

I don't know where the idea came from that being overweight is sexy. Let's look at it objectively, ladies, because this one is fully in your control.

Men are visual. That's not a secret, it's how we're wired. We respond to what we see first. If we're not drawn in visually, we're not going to fight for you.

Getting in shape will greatly increase your options. It can double, sometimes triple, the number of men who want you. That means a much bigger pool of men to choose from, and more of them will be men you would actually take seriously.

“But I love my curves, and guys still hit on me”

I hear you. But ask yourself two things. First, which guys are hitting on you, and are they the ones you want? Second, if losing the weight gave you more options and let you live a longer, healthier life, how would that make you worse off? It wouldn't. This isn't about hurting you. It's about helping you.

The slim friend test

If you have a slim friend, and when you go out you notice men literally walk around you to go talk to her, imagine you had a similar body type. Those men would be split between the two of you. How many more options would you have?

It's your body and your choice. But if what you want is more options, this is the fastest way to get them.

The bottom line

Men like a woman who's in shape. Getting there widens your pool, raises your confidence and improves your health at the same time.

Way Two

Dress Modestly

Let me tell you what men think when every outfit you go out in has to show some skin. It's not “relationship candidate.” It's not “I should take her on a date.”

It's: “Let me get her back to my place. Not much effort required.”

If that's how you want to be seen, that's your choice. But we're talking about raising the quality of your options. And remember Chapter Two: a man who only wants to sleep with you is worthless as an option.

Modesty is your force field

When you dress modestly, it works like a force field. The men with bad intentions see you and think: “Nah, she's work. She probably respects herself. She probably has a good relationship with her father.”

Just by how you dressed, about half of the low-intent men who would have approached you are gone. You filtered them out without saying a word.

It won't drop to zero. But you'll attract far fewer of the wrong men, and the ones who still step to you are more likely to see you as girlfriend or wife material.

The bottom line

How you dress decides who approaches you. Dress for the man you want, not the man who only wants one night.

Way Three

Act Like a Lady

Somewhere in the past fifty years, a lot of women were told that acting like a man would get them what they want. Has that worked for you?

It doesn't. Acting like a lady has worked for thousands of years, and it's not going to suddenly stop working now.

Let him protect you

Men naturally want to protect a woman. It's an instinct. But when you say, “Nah, I've got it, I don't need your help, I don't need you,” you switch that instinct off. He hears it, believes you, and goes to talk to the woman who looks like she'd appreciate his help.

If you want to be taken care of, you can't act like a man, because men don't get taken care of.

Your power isn't his power

I understand why acting like a man looks appealing. You see that men can sleep with whoever they want without emotional attachment and without being judged. But that's his power, not yours.

Your power is different, and honestly, it's stronger. Your power is getting men to do things for you without lifting a finger, just by being a lady.

The heavy box

Say you have to move a heavy box from point A to point B. You pick it up, struggle, set it down and say, “Wow, this is so heavy, I don't think I can move it.” All of a sudden three men appear out of nowhere: “I got it, I got it, I got it.” The box goes exactly where you wanted it. You didn't lift a finger.

Some people will call that weakness. It isn't. That's your power. But if you say, “I don't need your help, I've got it,” now you're struggling alone. For what? Pride.

The bottom line

When you act like a lady, more men, and the kind of men you want, will take you seriously.

Way Four

Your Body Count

The fourth way to raise your options, in quality and not just quantity: keep your body count low.

Here's what you should know about how men think. Most men understand they're not getting a woman with no past. They accept it. That's reality. But if you want to be taken seriously for the long term, the creator's view is that anything over nine or ten is too much for most men.

Already past that? Then don't act like it.

If you're already over that number, that's fine. It's not the end. The key is this: don't act like your body count. Drop the tendencies men read as red flags:

  • Don't announce everything you've done and everywhere you've been. “I did this, I did that, I went here, I went there” tells him more than you think.
  • Don't act like you've seen it all. Let him be the one to show you the world, like Aladdin.
  • Don't be out every week and every weekend.
  • Don't keep a bunch of guy friends around.

Men pick up on these habits instantly, and they shape whether he sees you as a girlfriend or a good time.

Not lying, pacing

I'm not telling you to lie to him. I'm telling you not to lead with your past. Some men won't like me for saying this, but I'm here for you right now, not them.

Share your past in small pieces, a little here and a little there, as he keeps investing in you. By the time he knows the full story, he's already in love with you, he looks past it and he forgives it.

Then close that chapter for good

Once he knows everything, leave that life behind for real. If you go back to those habits, you will end up alone.

The bottom line

Your past is your past. Your behavior now is what he's deciding on. Be the woman he'd commit to, and then stay her.

Chapter Nine

Operate in Reality

I've just laid out every way you can raise the quality of your options. You don't have to listen to me.

But if you don't, you might want to keep your phone on vibrate, because a ring isn't coming. You'll end up like James Harden, Russell Westbrook and Chris Paul: all that talent, no rings.

Here's the honest question. If doing things your way hasn't gotten you what you want, what do you have to lose by trying the blueprint?

What it all comes down to

If you really want what you say you want, a man who will take care of you, protect you and commit to you, then you have to:

  1. Operate in reality and let go of the illusion.
  2. Take an honest accounting of yourself and see who you are right now.
  3. Do the work it takes to attract the man you want.

If you stay stuck in your ways and don't think you need to change or grow, you could end up alone, with only the cats, the dogs and the books for company. You deserve better than that. Now you know how to get it.

Ask yourself
  • Of the men giving me attention right now, which ones are actually offering commitment?
  • Which of the four ways am I already doing, and which one would change the most for me?
  • What's one thing I can start this week?
