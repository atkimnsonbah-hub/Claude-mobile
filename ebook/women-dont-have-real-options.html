Chapter One

The Illusion of Abundance

A lot of women think they have all these options, and that those options will always be there. No matter how old they get, they believe they'll always have the same abundance of men who want them.

I always found that funny, because it doesn't work like that.

A woman might say:

I've got guys all over me. My phone, my DMs, my guy friends waiting in line, guys approaching me everywhere on the street. Of course I've got options.

And to that I say: no. Those options are not real.

Women have been making this mistake for thousands of years. I get it, but I also think it's pretty silly. Let me show you why.

Chapter Two

Wanting You Isn't Choosing You

The core mistake: women confuse sexual attraction with relationship attraction.

Just because a guy is interested in you doesn't make him a viable relationship candidate. Not at all. It has never meant that for men.

Men compartmentalize

If I want to sleep with you, I want to sleep with you. If I want a relationship with you, I want a relationship with you. Those are two different categories. Just because I want to sleep with you doesn't mean I want to wife you.

So you can't think, “He thinks I'm cute, so if things don't work out with my man I can always go to him.” No. You don't even know if he likes you. He may just want to hook up, and that doesn't mean anything.

Flip it around

Here's how it looks from our side. A man who wants to sleep with you but doesn't want to commit is the same thing as a woman who wants a relationship with me but isn't sexually attracted to me.

What am I supposed to do with that? It means absolutely nothing to me. That's not a real option.

It's the same for you. A man who wants to sleep with you but doesn't want to commit isn't a real option. Him wanting you is like saying water is wet. It's a given. It doesn't hold any real value.

Remember this

A man offering sex and a man offering commitment are two different things.

Chapter Three

Do the Math

Let's break the delusion with a little logic. How many options do you actually have?

Picture a thousand men who find you attractive, then run them through the filters that actually matter for a long-term relationship.

1,000 find you attractive
~900 would sleep with you
~500 would actually date you
Far fewer would marry you
Fewer still you also like

Some men find you attractive but still wouldn't touch you. Narrow it down. Of the ones who would, only some would take you on a date. Of those, only a fraction want to marry you. Then that fraction has to be men you are also into.

Yes, you can find guys to hook up with. Yes, you can find guys to date you. But guys for the long term, who want to marry you, and who you also like? All those filters shrink the pool very, very quickly.

The bottom line

From the thousand options you think you have, you only have a handful of real, viable, long-term options that actually mean something.

Chapter Four

It's Not a Replenishing Resource

I'm going to be honest with you. If you're in your mid-thirties to early forties, you've probably already met all the guys who could have been good candidates.

If you're still single now, it may be because you rejected them. You didn't realize you didn't have as many options as you thought. You turned them down, kept it casual, or ruined it. That ship sailed.

Yet a lot of women still think there are more coming, like it's a replenishing resource. Unfortunately for you, it doesn't work like that.

The difference between us

For men, there's a woman turning twenty-one every single day. From our side, the supply never ends.

For you, the older you get and the more money you make, the fewer men there are you'll consider. The pool keeps getting smaller and it gets harder and harder.

But I'm not here to give you doom and gloom. All is not lost. Here are the four ways to increase your real, quality, long-term options.

Way One

Get in Shape

I don't know where the idea came from that being overweight is somehow sexy. Let's think about it objectively, ladies.

Men are visual creatures, correct? We're stimulated by what we see. If you're visually unappealing, how likely are we to fight for you? Not very.

Getting in shape will greatly increase your options. It can double, sometimes triple, the number of guys who want you. That gives you a much bigger pool of men to choose from, men you'd actually want to take seriously.

“But I love my curves, and guys still hit on me”

Okay. First, who are those guys? Second, are you telling me that if you lost the weight and got more options, and lived longer because of it, you'd somehow be worse off? Really? We're not trying to hurt you. We're trying to help you.

The slim friend test

If you have a slim friend, and when you go out you notice men literally walk around you to go talk to her, imagine if you had a similar body type. Those men would be split between the two of you. How many more options would you have?

Be heavy if you want to. But if you really want to increase the number of options you have, lose the weight.

The bottom line

Men like a woman who's in shape. It's the fastest way to widen your pool and improve your health at the same time.

Way Two

Dress Modestly

If every outfit you wear when you go out has to show some skin, you know what men like me think? Not “relationship candidate.” Not “I should take her on a date.”

We think, “Let me get her back to my place. Not much effort required.”

If that's how you want to be seen, fine. But we're talking about increasing the quality of your options, and remember: a man who only wants to sleep with you is worthless as an option.

Modesty is a force field

Dressing modestly acts like a force field. The men without the best intentions see you and think: “Nah, that one's work. She probably respects herself. She probably has a good relationship with her father.”

Just by how you dressed, half of the low-intent men who would have approached you are gone. You're too much work for them.

It's not going to drop to zero. But the number you attract will be much lower, and the men who remain are the ones thinking of you as girlfriend or wife material.

The bottom line

How you dress decides who approaches. Dress for the man you want, not the man who wants one night.

Way Three

Act Like a Lady

I don't know exactly what happened, but in the past fifty years a lot of women decided that acting like a man would get them what they want. How's that working?

It doesn't. Acting like a lady has worked for thousands of years. It's not going to suddenly stop working now.

Men are built to protect

Men naturally want to protect a woman. It's an instinct. But if a woman says, “Nah, I've got it, I don't need your help, I don't need you,” then we won't want to protect her. You don't want to be taken care of? Go ahead and do it all by yourself. I'm going to talk to the girl over there who looks like she could use my help.

If you want to be taken care of, you can't act like a man, because men don't get taken care of.

Your power vs. our power

I understand why acting like a man looks attractive. You see that men can sleep with whoever we want without emotional attachment and without being judged. But that's our power.

Your power is different. Your power is getting men to do things for you without lifting a finger, just by being a lady.

The heavy box

Say you have to move a heavy box from point A to point B. You grab it, struggle, set it down and say, “Wow, this is so heavy, I don't think I can move it.” All of a sudden three guys materialize: “I got it, I got it, I got it.” The box goes exactly where you wanted it. You didn't lift a finger.

Some women would call that weakness. It isn't. That's your power. But if you say, “I don't need your help, I've got it,” now you're struggling. For what? Pride.

The bottom line

If you act like a lady, more men, and most men want exactly that, will take you seriously.

Way Four

Your Body Count

The fourth way to increase your options, in quality and not just quantity: keep your body count low.

Most men understand we're not getting a woman with no past. We accept that. It's reality. But if you want to be taken seriously long term, my view is that anything over nine or ten is too much.

Already over? Then don't act like it.

Let's be honest, plenty of women reading this are already past that number. Fine. The key is this: don't act like your body count. That means no hoe tendencies:

  • Don't announce everything you've done and everywhere you've been. “I did this, I did that, I went here, I went there” is a hoe tendency.
  • Don't act like you've been there before. Let him be the one to show you the world, like Aladdin.
  • Don't be out every week and every weekend.
  • Don't keep a bunch of guy friends.

These are the habits of women with high body counts, and men read them instantly.

Not lying, pacing

I'm not saying lie to him. What I'm saying is: don't act like you have that past. Men won't like me saying this, but I'm helping the women right now.

Tell him about your past in small pieces, a bit here and a bit there, while he keeps investing in you. By the time he knows the full story, he's already in love with you, looks past it and forgives you for it.

Then close that chapter for good

Once he knows your full past, don't do any of that again. If you go back to those habits, you will end up alone.

The bottom line

Your past is your past. Your behavior is what he's deciding on. Act like the woman he'd commit to, and then stay her.

Chapter Nine

Operate in Reality

I've just laid out every way you can increase the quality of your options. You don't have to listen to me.

But if you choose not to, keep your phone on vibrate, because you're not getting a ring. You'll end up like James Harden, Russell Westbrook and Chris Paul: all that talent, no rings.

Here's the obvious part. If you've noticed that doing whatever you want isn't working, maybe it's time to try the blueprint. Take my word for it, or don't.

What it all comes down to

If you really want what you say you want, a man who'll take care of you, protect you and all of that, you have to:

  1. Operate in reality and get out of the delusion.
  2. Take an honest accounting of yourself and realize who you are right now.
  3. Understand the work it takes to get the man you want, and do it.

If you're stuck in your ways and don't think you need to change or improve, then you'll be alone tomorrow, unless you count the cats, the dogs and the books.
