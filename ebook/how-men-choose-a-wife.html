Introduction

The Question You're Not Asking

“I've got options.” — The most comfortable lie a woman can tell herself.

Your phone is never quiet. There are messages in your DMs, guy friends who'd drop everything if you called, men who stop you on the street to tell you you're beautiful. Every week someone new is trying.

So when you think about your love life, there's a voice in the back of your head that says: I'm good. If this one doesn't work out, there's always another one.

But here's the question you're not asking: of all those men, how many are actually offering you what you want? Not attention. Not a good night. Commitment. A ring. A future.

I usually give men advice. Today I'm talking to you. You've told me I'm too hard on women, so this time I'm on your side of the table. I'm going to tell you what men actually think, the stuff we say to each other and never say to you, so you can stop guessing and start choosing.

The options you think you have aren't real. But you can build real ones, if you know how men actually choose.

This book will break the illusion first. Then it will give you the blueprint.

Let's get into it.

Chapter One

Attention vs. Real Options

From where you're standing, they look identical. That's exactly why so many women get this wrong.

Most women believe their options will always be there, and that no matter how old they get, there will always be men who want them. Women have been making this mistake for thousands of years, and I understand why. From your side, attention looks exactly like opportunity.

It isn't. Here's the core mistake: you're confusing sexual attraction with relationship attraction.

How Men Really Think

Men compartmentalize. When a man wants to sleep with you, that's what he wants. When a man wants a relationship with you, that's a completely different thing. Two separate boxes. A man wanting you in bed does not mean he wants to make you his wife.

So when you think, “He thinks I'm cute, so if things don't work out with my man I can always go to him,” stop right there. You don't even know if he likes you. He may just want to hook up. That's not a backup plan. That's nothing.

A Real Option

  • Offers commitment, not just attention
  • Sees you as girlfriend or wife material
  • Wants to take you on a real date
  • Thinks of a future with you
  • Is someone you actually want back

Just Attention

  • Offers sex, not commitment
  • Sees you as “not much effort required”
  • Wants to get you back to his place
  • Thinks about tonight, not next year
  • Is only a “backup” in your head

You Already Know This Feeling

Think about the guy who's sweet to you, wants a relationship with you, would commit tomorrow, but you're just not attracted to him. Does he count as an option to you? Be honest. No. You'd never actually choose him.

That's exactly how men see it from the other side. The man who wants to sleep with you but won't commit is your version of that guy. His wanting you is like saying water is wet. Of course he does. It doesn't hold any real value for you.

Remember this

A man offering sex and a man offering commitment are two different things.

The Bottom Line

Attention is not the same as options. You can be surrounded by men and still have very few who would actually choose you, and even fewer you'd choose back.

Chapter Two

The Reality of the Numbers

Put a little logic on it, and the pool gets very small, very fast.

Let's do the math. Picture a thousand men who find you attractive. Now run them through the filters that matter to you, if what you want is a real, long-term relationship.

1,000 find you attractive
~900 would sleep with you
~500 would actually date you
10 want to marry you
You only find 3 of them attractive

Some men will find you attractive and still pass. Of the ones who wouldn't, only some would take you on a real date. Of those, only about ten want to marry you. And of those ten, you only find three of them attractive.

Yes, you can find guys to hook up with. Yes, you can find guys to date you. But a man who wants you for the long term, wants to marry you, and is someone you like back? All those filters shrink the pool very, very quickly.

Out of the thousand options you think you have, you have about three that actually mean something.

It's Not a Replenishing Resource

This part is hard to hear, so I'll say it honestly. If you're in your mid-thirties to early forties, there's a good chance you've already met the men who could have been great partners for you.

If you're still single, it may be because you passed on them. Not because you're a bad person, but because you believed you had more options than you did. Maybe you turned them down, kept it casual, or let it fall apart. Those chances may be gone.

The mistake is thinking there are always more coming, like the supply refills on its own. Unfortunately, for you it doesn't work like that. For men, there's a woman turning twenty-one every single day. From his side, the supply never runs out. For you, the older you get and the more money you make, the fewer men there are you'll consider. Your standards go up while the pool gets smaller.

The Mindset Shift

Stop treating your options like they're endless. Treat the few real ones like the rare thing they are, and start doing the things that put more of them in front of you.

Chapter Three

The 4 Ways to Raise Your Options

Not more attention. Better men. Four moves that change who shows up for you.

I'm not here to give you doom and gloom. All is not lost. Some men won't like me for this, because it works against their strategy, but I'm on your side today. Here are the four ways to increase your real, quality, long-term options, starting now.

Way One

Get in Shape

I don't know where the idea came from that being overweight is sexy. Let's look at it objectively, ladies, because this one is fully in your control.

Men are visual. That's not a secret, it's how we're wired. We respond to what we see first, and if we're not drawn in visually, we're not going to fight for you. Getting in shape will greatly increase your options. It can double, sometimes triple, the number of men who want you, and more of them will be men you would actually take seriously.

“But I love my curves, and guys still hit on me.” I hear you. Ask yourself which guys, and whether they're the ones you want. And if losing the weight gave you more options and a longer, healthier life, how would that make you worse off? It wouldn't.

Here's the test. If you have a slim friend, and when you go out men literally walk around you to go talk to her, imagine you had a similar body type. Those men would be split between the two of you. It's your body and your choice. But if you want more options, this is the fastest way to get them.

Way Two

Dress Modestly

Let me tell you what men think when every outfit you go out in has to show some skin. It's not “relationship candidate.” It's not “I should take her on a date.” It's: “Let me get her back to my place. Not much effort required.”

If that's how you want to be seen, that's your choice. But we're talking about the quality of your options, and a man who only wants to sleep with you is worthless as an option.

When you dress modestly, it works like a force field. The men with bad intentions see you and think: “Nah, she's work. She probably respects herself. She probably has a good relationship with her father.” Just by how you dressed, about half of the low-intent men who would have approached you are gone.

It won't drop to zero. But you'll attract far fewer of the wrong men, and the ones who still step to you are more likely to see you as girlfriend or wife material. Dress for the man you want, not the man who only wants one night.

Way Three

Act Like a Lady

Somewhere in the past fifty years, a lot of women were told that acting like a man would get them what they want. Has that worked for you? Acting like a lady has worked for thousands of years, and it's not going to suddenly stop working now.

Men naturally want to protect a woman. It's an instinct. But when you say, “Nah, I've got it, I don't need your help, I don't need you,” you switch that instinct off. He believes you, and he goes to talk to the woman who looks like she'd appreciate his help. If you want to be taken care of, you can't act like a man, because men don't get taken care of.

Sleeping around without attachment and without judgment is his power, not yours. Your power is different, and honestly, it's stronger: getting men to do things for you without lifting a finger. Say you have to move a heavy box. You struggle, set it down and say, “Wow, this is so heavy, I don't think I can move it.” Suddenly three men appear: “I got it, I got it, I got it.”

Some people call that weakness. It isn't. That's your power. But if you say, “I don't need your help,” now you're struggling alone. For what? Pride.

Way Four

Keep Your Body Count Low, and Don't Act Like It

Most men understand they're not getting a woman with no past. They accept it. But if you want to be taken seriously for the long term, the view here is that anything over nine or ten is too much for most men.

If you're already past that number, it's not the end. The key is this: don't act like your body count. Don't lead with everything you've done and everywhere you've been. Let him be the one to show you the world, like Aladdin.

I'm not telling you to lie to him. I'm telling you to pace it. Share your past in small pieces, a little here and a little there, as he keeps investing in you. By the time he knows the full story, he's already in love with you, he looks past it and he forgives it.

Then close that chapter for good. Once he knows everything, leave that life behind for real. If you go back to those habits, you will end up alone.

Red Flag Checklist: Habits Men Read Instantly

  • Announcing everything you've done: “I did this, I did that, I went here, I went there.”
  • Acting like you've seen it all and nothing is new to you.
  • Being out every week and every weekend.
  • Keeping a bunch of guy friends around.
  • Telling him “I don't need your help” when he steps up.

The Bottom Line

Get in shape, dress modestly, act like a lady, and keep your past in its place. Each one filters out the men who only want attention, and puts you in front of the men who want commitment.

Final Words

Operate in Reality.

I've just laid out every way you can raise the quality of your options. You don't have to listen to me. But if you don't, you might want to keep your phone on vibrate, because a ring isn't coming. You'll end up like James Harden, Russell Westbrook and Chris Paul: all that talent, no rings.

Here's the honest question. If doing things your way hasn't gotten you what you want, what do you have to lose by trying the blueprint?

If you really want a man who will take care of you, protect you and commit to you, it comes down to three things. Operate in reality and let go of the illusion. Take an honest accounting of yourself and see who you are right now. And do the work it takes to attract the man you want.

If you stay stuck in your ways, you could end up alone, with only the cats, the dogs and the books for company. You deserve better than that. And now you know how to get it.

Attention is easy to get. Commitment is earned. Build yourself for the man who commits.
